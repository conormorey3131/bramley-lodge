Family-run since 2009

Wholesome food, handed-down recipes, the freshest local produce

We take the very best locally sourced produce and apply our craft as only we can — nothing added but a dollop of skill and a generous sprinkling of passion.

Working closely with local farmers and artisans, we serve breakfast through dinner every day of the week — with everything baked in-house daily.
